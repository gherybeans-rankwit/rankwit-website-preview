How it works

From the form to the report

There is nothing to install. We need two minutes of your time now and thirty at the end.

  1. You tell us who you areHotel or destination, your website and how to reach you. That is enough to build the question set.
  2. We build the questionsDiscovery, planning and booking questions for your category and destination, in the languages of your markets. Usually around fifty.
  3. We run them on five enginesEvery question, on ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI Overviews. We keep every answer in full.
  4. You get the reportWhere you are named, where you are missing, the sources, the first three actions. We go through it with you in a short call if you want one.

Questions

Before you send the form

Is it really free?
Yes. The audit is how we start every conversation, because the numbers make the case better than we can. If you want to keep measuring after it, we tell you what that costs. If not, the report stays yours.
Who is it for?
Hotels, whether independent or part of a group, and destinations such as DMOs, tourism boards and consortia. If you sell nights, or represent a territory that does, the audit is for you.
What do you need from me?
Only the form. If you want to go further, knowing who you compete with and your main markets helps us ask the right questions. We do not need access to your site or your accounts for the audit.
