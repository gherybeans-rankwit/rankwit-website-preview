Questions

When will I see results?
No sooner than 90 days, and often longer. The engines do not re-read the web every day. A page you fix or earn has to be crawled, then picked up by the review sites and guides the models rely on, and only then does it start to change the answers. Each of those steps takes weeks. You will see the first movements earlier in the weekly measurements, but we only call it a result when you are named consistently for the questions that matter, and that takes at least three months.
Can I keep my current agency?
Yes. We measure and prepare what to change, then we put it online or your agency does, whichever you prefer. Where nobody can work on the site, RankWit Edge applies the fixes without changing it.
What does being named bring?
For a hotel, a guest who gets your name from an AI answer has usually already decided, and books with you directly. OTA commissions run between 15 and 25 percent, so a few of those bookings a month often cover what the service costs. For a destination, being named is where the trip starts: visitors come looking for the places the answer gave them, and that demand reaches the hotels in your area.
