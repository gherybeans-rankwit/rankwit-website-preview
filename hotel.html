What you lose without noticing

An AI engine does not tell a hotel when it leaves it out. It happens in three ways.

3 or 4 names

The guest who was given other names

An AI answer gives three or four hotels and stops there. When yours is not among them, the guest does not go looking for you. They look up the hotel they were given.

15 to 25%

The booking that went through an OTA

When an engine does name you, it often links to your listing on a booking platform rather than your own site. The guest books there, and the commission comes off the top of a booking that was already yours.

1 old review

The claim nobody corrected

An engine can repeat for months that you have no restaurant, or that you close in winter, because one old review said so. Until someone corrects the source, guests keep reading it.

Questions from hotels

Is this SEO?
No. SEO competes for one of ten links on a results page. An AI answer names three or four hotels and stops there, so we measure whether you are one of them and work on the pages that decide it.
Does it work for a small independent hotel?
Yes. We compare you with the hotels you actually compete with. A small hotel with a strong reputation on review sites is often already named and does not know it.
What about my OTA listings?
They are sources too. Engines cite booking platforms and review sites heavily. We show you when they cite your listing there and not your own site.
When will I see results?
No sooner than 90 days, and often longer. A page you fix or earn has to be crawled, then picked up by the review sites and guides the models rely on, and only then does it start to change the answers. Each step takes weeks. You will see the first movements earlier in the weekly measurements, but a result means being named consistently for the questions that matter, and that takes at least three months.
How long does onboarding take?
Your competitors and your questions are set in the first week. The first answers arrive with the first weekly measurement.
